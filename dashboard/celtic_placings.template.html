Celtic League · Pro12 · Pro14 · URC — final placings

Regular-season finishing position of every club, by season. Hover a point for the record; click a club (line or legend) to pin it; click again to clear.

Pinned: none hollow = season not fully covered by the data
  • Tables are rebuilt from the raw match files (4 for a win, 2 for a draw, +1 losing bonus for a loss by ≤7, +1 for ≥4 tries), ordered by points, then wins, then points difference. Playoffs are excluded, so this is regular-season position, not the champion.
  • Try bonus points are missing before 2011-12 (no try events recorded), so close finishes in 2003-11 may differ from the official table.
  • Hollow markers: 2006-07, 2013-14, 2014-15 and 2025-26 have only part of the season's results in the data (2012-13, 2015-16, 2017-18, 2018-19 are missing a game or so per team); positions are standings at the end of the data available.
  • 2020-21 (two conferences plus the Rainbow Cup) and the single 2002-03 match are not shown.
